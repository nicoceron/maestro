# Product direction

Maestro reaches behavioral parity with My Music Staff while deliberately replacing several inherited constraints.

## Principles

1. One global identity with instant, safe studio switching.
2. A calm role-specific home screen: the next action, not a wall of modules.
3. Offline-capable teacher days for attendance, notes, mileage, and practice media.
4. A real two-way family inbox across email, SMS, in-app, and optional WhatsApp.
5. A transparent financial ledger with split/business/sponsor payers and explicit allocations.
6. Recurrence and rescheduling that preview capacity, billing, payroll, and notifications before commit.
7. Program/service policies scoped by teacher, location, and offering rather than business-wide toggles.
8. Complete imports, exports, audit history, consent, retention, and tenant restore.
9. WCAG 2.2 AA, keyboard workflows, and responsive/offline behavior as release gates.
10. Clean-room branding, copy, information architecture, and visual design.

## Intentional simplifications

- Build excellent scheduling, teaching, billing, communication, and migration before a full drag-and-drop CMS.
- Use one event/occurrence engine rather than separate lesson, cancellation, make-up, and opening silos.
- Use shared people/contact primitives with role profiles rather than duplicating teacher, parent, and student contact logic.
- Use one notification/template pipeline for every channel.
- Use one attachments/media subsystem with explicit tenant ownership and access rules.

## Differentiators after core parity

- Constraint-ranked scheduling and substitute suggestions.
- A make-up marketplace across configurable teacher pools.
- Lead pipeline, trial conversion, abandoned-booking recovery, and automation rules.
- Practice goals, recording feedback threads, curriculum progress, and parent digests.
- Drill-down analytics for revenue, A/R, retention, utilization, conversion, attendance, make-up liability, teacher economics, and engagement.
- Public versioned API, idempotent webhooks, and no-code tenant automation.
